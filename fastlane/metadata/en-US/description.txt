Morser is a Morse code trainer for the Mac.

It is built around copying by ear rather than reading dots and dashes off a
screen. Characters are sent as tones at a speed you set, and you type what you
hear.

FEATURES

• Practice drills — common words, callsigns, and plain text at your pace
• Pileup — copy one station out of a crowd of them calling at once, the way a
  real contest sounds
• Keyer — send with your own fist and see how cleanly it decodes
• Alphabet song — hear the whole character set in order while it sinks in
• Timing diagnostics — see your dot/dash ratios and where your spacing drifts

Every element has its own pitch, so characters stay distinguishable as speed
goes up.
